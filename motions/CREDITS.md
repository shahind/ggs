# Motion credits

GGS's animation library comes from these sources. When you ship a game that uses one of these motions, put the
matching line in your credits.

| Catalog | Credit line | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Starter set (`motions/clips`) | "Animations made with NVIDIA Kimodo" (optional: NVIDIA claims no rights in outputs) | NVIDIA Open Model License (the model); the motions are yours |
| CMU (`motions/catalogs/cmu.zip`) | "The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217." | CMU: free for use in research and commercial projects |
| 100STYLE (`motions/catalogs/100style.zip`) | "The 100STYLE Dataset - Ian Mason" | CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

Animations you make with "Describe a motion" are made by Kimodo on your computer and are yours.

The CMU motions were converted to BVH by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed.com); GGS converted those to 2D bone angles for its
side-view rig. 100STYLE clips were trimmed with the dataset's Frame_Cuts.csv and cut into seamless loops. Mason,
Starke & Komura, "Real-Time Style Modelling of Human Locomotion via Feature-Wise Transformations and Local Motion
Phases", ACM TOG 2022, doi:10.1145/3522618.
